Cannibal Cave

An open-minded, loud-hearted, fire-first steakhouse concept from Matthew Carpenter. Industrial atmosphere, primal appetite, honest plates, and a room that feels like steel, smoke, and good decisions made slightly too late.

A steakhouse with bite.

Cannibal Cave is not gore. It is hunger, craft, and heat. A playful industrial steakhouse built around open-fire cooking, heavy music energy, honest hospitality, and the kind of atmosphere that makes dinner feel like an event.

Fire First

Char, smoke, cast iron, flame, and bold seasoning. Food that tastes like it survived something useful.

Industrial Room

Black steel, ember light, concrete texture, red glow, warm tables, and a little CRT grime in the soul.

Positive Chaos

Bubbly service, weird branding, serious execution. Fun without becoming fake.
